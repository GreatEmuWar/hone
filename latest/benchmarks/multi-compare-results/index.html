    Noise with several objectives: each reserve against believing every draw¶

    Each reserve is compared with the baseline (every draw believed), paired by seed. Criterion 1: pooled and per-run mean share of reported points that truly break a limit, each at most 10%; empty fronts are counted apart and are not passes. Criterion 2: the share of runs holding a truly feasible point rises where the baseline's is under 90% (unless the cell is seed-for-seed unchanged) and falls by no more than one run in ten. Criterion 3: d = reserve's true gap less baseline's; L and U are the 11th and 20th smallest of 30; pass if U is at most δ (2% of the true front's scaled hypervolume), fail if L is above δ, else unresolved. In a cell the floor can't resolve, an unresolved criterion 3 does not count against a reserve; a fail does. Criteria 4 and 5 are checked by tests and the cost harness, not from these runs, and the verdict assumes they hold. The alone runs are for the record.
