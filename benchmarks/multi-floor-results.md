## The paired noise floor

For each seed the difference d is the paired run's true gap less the baseline's, so a positive d means the paired run is worse. L and U are the order statistics that bracket the median of the d's with 95% confidence (the 11th and 20th smallest of 30). A cell resolves δ, 2% of the true front's scaled hypervolume, when U is at most δ, L is at least -δ and U - L is at most δ; otherwise the benchmark can't resolve δ there. Gaps are in scaled hypervolume. The baseline's own figures follow.

A cell that resolves δ is one where a real no-change would pass criterion 3 and a real loss of 2δ or more would fail it (about 1.5δ when the interval is centred on zero, as it should be here); a loss between δ and that comes out unresolved. The bounds are one draw of a random interval, so the verdict is a guide, not a guarantee. For random search the floor is wider than the real comparison's spread, since there the default and the baseline propose the same designs, so a random-search cell that can't resolve δ here may still resolve in the comparison.
